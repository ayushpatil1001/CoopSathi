# CoopSathi AI — Backlink Strategy

## Objective
Build high-authority, DoFollow backlinks from official government and cooperative sector portals to `coopsathi.gov.in`.

---

## Tier 1 — Official Government Portals (Highest Priority)

| Target Domain | DA | Action |
|:---|:---|:---|
| cooperation.gov.in | 55+ | Request Ministry webmaster to link from their "Digital Initiatives" page |
| crcs.gov.in | 50+ | Link from CRCS digital grievance section to our Ombudsman page |
| india.gov.in | 85+ | Submit for listing under "e-Governance Initiatives" on National Portal |
| digitalindia.gov.in | 70+ | Apply for listing under Digital India ecosystem partners |
| mygov.in | 75+ | Create official campaign page on MyGov linking to CoopSathi |
| nabard.org | 60+ | Request NABARD to reference CoopSathi for PACS computerization queries |
| pmfby.gov.in | 55+ | Request cross-link from PMFBY farmer resources page to our PMFBY guide |

## Tier 3 — State Government Portals

Target state agriculture/cooperation department websites in:
- Maharashtra, Uttar Pradesh, Rajasthan, Gujarat, Karnataka, Punjab, MP, AP, Telangana

Each state's cooperation department should reference the national cooperative portal.

### High-Value Content Assets to Create
1. **"Complete Guide to Cooperative Registration in India 2024"** — Target keyword: "how to register cooperative society India"
2. **"PMFBY Crop Insurance: 72-Hour Intimation Complete Guide"** — Target: "pmfby claim intimation process"
3. **"PACS ERP: 25+ Services Available at Your Local PACS"** — Target: "PACS services list India"
4. **"KCC Interest Subvention 4%: Complete Eligibility Guide"** — Target: "kisan credit card interest rate 2024"

### Outreach Channels
- **Press Release Distribution**: Submit to PIB (pib.gov.in) for official government press coverage.
- **Cooperative Journals**: Publish articles in "Cooperative Perspectives" (NCCT publication).
- **Agriculture Portals**: Syndicate content on Krishijagran.com, AgriHunt.com.
- **Regional Language SEO**: Hindi cooperative content targeting vernacular search queries.

## Schema & Structured Data for Link Equity
Ensure all pages have `sameAs` linking to `cooperation.gov.in` and `crcs.gov.in` in GovernmentOrganization schema to signal authority.
