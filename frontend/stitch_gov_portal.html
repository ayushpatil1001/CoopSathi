Historic Cabinet Decision

Three New National Level Multi-State Cooperative Societies

Empowering PACS as primary shareholders to boost export, organic farming, and certified seed multiplication.

yard
1. National Seed Cooperative

Bharatiya Beej Sahakari Samiti Ltd (BBSSL)

Multiplication, testing, and distribution of high-yield certified quality seeds by PACS farmers.

eco
2. National Organic Cooperative

National Cooperative Organics Ltd (NCOL)

Certified testing, branding ('Bharat Organics'), and marketing for fair organic farmer prices.

public
3. National Export Cooperative

National Cooperative Exports Ltd (NCEL)

Facilitating agricultural surplus export directly into global markets with direct farmer profit return.
